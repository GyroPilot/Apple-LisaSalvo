16 note alert
How to play^L^L
Pick a table: the AI tables put you against the
server's admirals; the others wait for people.
Press R when you are ready.  When the game
starts, place your five ships (5, 4, 3, 3 and 2
squares) on your fleet's sea, on the left: click
a square for each, SPACE turns them, A places
all at random, RETURN sends them.^L^L
Then take turns firing: click a square of the
target sea.  One shot hits every other fleet at
that square.  Black = a hit, a dot = a miss.
A fleet with nothing afloat is out; the last
fleet afloat wins.  45 seconds a turn.
