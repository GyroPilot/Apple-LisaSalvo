LisaSalvo 0.3 for the Apple Lisa Office System       October 2026 (release build REV H)
Battleship (Salvo) on the FujiNet Battleship server.  Tool 419322.

PLAY
  Needs an RS-232 FujiNet on Serial B.  LisaSalvo lists the tables on Eric Carr's public
  FujiNet Battleship server (battleship.carr-designs.com): play against computer admirals
  ("ai" tables) or people on other FujiNet machines.  Type a table's number to sit down.

    R  ready                 A  place your ships at random
    SPACE  turn a ship while placing      U  take back a ship
    RETURN  send your fleet / fire at the cursor
    F  the YOUR TURN line flashes on your turn; F turns the flash off and on
    L  leave the table       H  help

  On your turn, click a square of the target sea to fire.  Your ships are drawn on your own
  sea; a hit is a white hole with an X.  When the game ends, the final board stays on the
  screen until you press R for the next game.
